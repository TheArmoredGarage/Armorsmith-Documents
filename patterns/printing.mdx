<Info>
These instructions are for **Armorsmith Designer 2.1.1.0**. Earlier versions, including 2.1.0.10, can show different panels and labels.
</Info>

Once you have arranged your pattern pieces, check the page layout and print a test page before printing the full set. A printer scaling setting can change the size of every piece, so verify the printed result with a ruler.

## Prepare the pages

![Flat chest and trim pieces arranged across Letter pages with 5 mm margins in Paper and Printing.](/images/workspace-paper-layout-2110.png)

*Arrange the flat pieces on pages and check paper size and margins before printing.*


1. Review the pattern pieces in the 2D view. Make sure they are arranged on the pages you intend to print.
2. Select the workspace and open the relevant settings in **Workbench → Inspect**.
3. Use **Folds & Labels** for edge information, **Assembly Tabs** for tabs, and **Paper & Printing** for page numbers. Turn on the details you need, such as **Show Page Numbers**, **Show Registration Marks**, **Show Edge Angles**, **Show Edge IDs**, **Draw Solid Folds**, **Show Tabs**, or **Show Textures**.
4. Check any piece that sits near a page boundary. Use [Edit patterns](/patterns/pattern-layout) to move pieces or pack the layout again if needed.

<Tip>
Turn on page numbers to identify the sheets. Registration marks and edge IDs help line up corresponding pattern edges when assembling the pieces.
</Tip>

## Print a test page

![PDF viewer print dialog with Letter paper and Actual size selected alongside the chest pattern preview.](/images/pdf-actual-size-2110.png)

*When printing an exported PDF, choose Actual size or 100% instead of Fit to printable area. Measure a test print before cutting.*


<Steps>
  <Step title="Open printing">
    Click **Print** on the toolbar above the pattern view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check printer settings">
    Choose the printer and paper that match the pages you plan to use. If you first saved a PDF, open it in your PDF viewer and choose Print there. In the viewer or printer settings, select **100%** or **Actual Size**. Disable options such as **Fit to Page** that resize the output.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print and measure one page">
    Print a page containing a feature whose size you can check. Measure it with a ruler and compare it with the dimension shown in Armorsmith. If the measurements differ, correct the printer scaling settings before printing the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print the remaining pages">
    Once the test page measures correctly, print the rest of the layout. Keep the page numbers visible as you sort and assemble the sheets.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Printer scaling can make an otherwise correct pattern too large or too small. Check the size on paper before cutting your material.
</Warning>

## After printing

Lay out sheets in page order and align pattern lines that continue across page boundaries. Registration marks help line up corresponding pattern edges, rather than acting as page-corner alignment targets. Confirm that matching edges and fold details are visible before cutting the pieces.

For paper setup, the Print Mismatch prompt, and a measured test-page procedure, follow [Print your first full-size template](/getting-started/print-first-template).
