<Info>
These instructions are for **Armorsmith Designer 2.1.1.0**. Earlier versions, including 2.1.0.10, can show different panels and labels.
</Info>

Open **Workshop** to work on a workspace's model and flat patterns. Earlier guides call this area Pattern or Patterns. Choose a workspace under **Workbench → Browse → Workspaces** before editing it.

## Find your way around

| Area | What you use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **3D model view** | Inspect the source model, paint islands, or review formed foam. |
| **Pattern viewport** | Edit and arrange the flat pieces you will print. |
| **Pattern layout toolbar** | Align, distribute, and rotate pieces on the pages. |
| **Browse → Patterns** | Find drawing layers, groups, and shapes in the active workspace. |
| **Edit** | Work with the active tool's controls, including Edit Shape and Edit Foam. |
| **Inspect** | Edit selected-object or workspace properties. |
| **Transforms** | Enter precise position, rotation, or scale values where available. |

For an editable low-polygon model, Workshop shows the model beside its patterns. The paper area can remain hidden for a dense source without pattern content. Display LOD does not automatically make a dense source suitable for every pattern-editing operation.

## Choose the right pattern workflow

- [Islands](/patterns/create-and-manage-islands) mark areas on a source model; their patterns update as you edit those areas.
- **Unfold** generates an automatic layout from an appropriate model.
- [Drawing layers and groups](/patterns/layers-and-groups) organize shapes, images, and other drafting content.
- [Foam patterns](/foam-preview/overview) start from closed outlines and show a formed 3D design beside the flat cut shapes.

Drawing layers organize content. Foam layers place raised pieces at a physical height above a foam base; they are edited in **Edit Foam → Layers**.

## Show the details you need

Select the workspace, then open **Inspect**. Use **Pattern Appearance** for general appearance, **Folds & Labels** for edge labels and registration marks, **Assembly Tabs** for tabs, and **Paper & Printing** for page size, margins, and page numbers.

Hide unnecessary details while arranging pieces, then turn on the information you need for assembly before printing.

## Prepare a layout

1. Choose the workspace containing the model or patterns.
2. Generate or edit the required pieces.
3. Arrange them on the paper with the [pattern layout tools](/patterns/pattern-layout).
4. Check paper size, labels, and margins in Inspect.
5. [Print a measured test page](/getting-started/print-first-template).

For a first exercise, follow [Prepare a fitted piece for pattern layout](/getting-started/first-pattern-layout).
