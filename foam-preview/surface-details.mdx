<Info>
These instructions describe the upcoming **Armorsmith Designer 2.1.1.0** interface. The public 2.1.0.10 release can show different panels and labels.
</Info>

The **Printable Details** entry is withheld from the documented 2.1.1.0 workflow. Decorative rivet, eyelet, and washer placement is not available through that entry point.

Use [foam layers](/foam-preview/layers-and-materials) for raised shapes that bend with a base. Plan real hardware and any required holes separately; an eyelet's own opening would not automatically cut or mark a hole through foam.

For a supported starting exercise, follow the [layered bracer tutorial](/foam-preview/tutorial-layered-bracer).
