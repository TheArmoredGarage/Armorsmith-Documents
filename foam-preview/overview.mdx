Start with the shape you want to cut from a flat sheet of foam. Draw its outline in 2D, give it a thickness, then form the resulting piece in the left-hand 3D preview on the **Patterns** tab. Keep editing the same outline as you refine the design. Use **Attach as part** when you want the piece to appear in the main **Costume** view.

## Work from the cut pattern to the costume

1. Draw the outline using **Line**, **Rectangle**, or **Circle** in **Create 2D**.
2. Use right-click **Edit Shape** to refine it. Once closed, right-click **Promote to foam pattern** to create the 3D foam piece.
3. **Thickness** gives the piece its physical depth.
4. **Form** previews horizontal bending, vertical bending, or forming over a sphere.
5. **Place** positions and rotates the piece in 3D.
6. **Layers** adds smaller patterns that follow the same forming.
7. **Edge Style** previews angle cuts or rounded top edges.
8. **Details** attaches decorative dome rivets, eyelets, and washers.
9. **Attach as part** shows the completed foam stack in the main costume view. Toggle it off to keep working only in the Patterns preview.

The 2D outline remains your flat cut template. Forming, edge finishes, materials, and decorative hardware change the 3D preview without replacing that outline.

## Understand the preview

Single-piece forming is a geometric preview. Glued assemblies add a bounded foam relaxation pass that resists stretching, keeps connected edge samples together, and accounts for thickness, stiffness, and heat softness. Use it to judge construction, placement, layering, and appearance. It does not predict temperatures, glue curing, self-collision, gravity, or springback.

This preview includes drawing, editing, linked and mirrored instances, live half-outline symmetry, gluing, forming, layering, edge, and detail tools. Offset outlines, trim/extend, general spline joining, automatic seam allowances, darts, and notches are outside this documented preview. Existing tools for other pattern types have their own behavior.

For paper layout and print scale, continue with [Print patterns](/patterns/printing).
