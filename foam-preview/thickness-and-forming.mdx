Once you close an outline, its foam preview appears in the 3D costume view. Use **Foam** to show or hide the preview. The piece starts flat with a thickness of 6 mm.

## Match your foam stock

Select the pattern and click **Thickness**. Enter the thickness in millimeters. Each pattern keeps its own thickness, including patterns in the same layer.

Choose the thickness before judging edge finishes and stack height. A thicker piece changes the amount of material available for an angle cut and the height of patterns above it.

## Choose a forming shape

Open **Form** and choose a mode:

| Mode | Typical use | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Flat** | Flat plates, reference layout, or resetting a formed piece | No bend. |
| **Horizontal bend** | Wrap the pattern across its flat X direction | Bend angle in degrees. |
| **Vertical bend** | Wrap the pattern across its flat Y direction | Bend angle in degrees. |
| **Spherical form** | Dome-like armor or a curved shoulder detail | Sphere radius in millimeters. |

Horizontal and vertical refer to the pattern's flat axes. Rotating the piece in 3D rotates those axes with it.

A larger bend angle produces more wrap across the pattern. A smaller sphere radius produces tighter curvature. **Reverse forming direction** changes which way the surface curves.

<Note>
These controls preview the shape you might create by heat forming. They do not simulate temperature, foam stretching, or springback. Test spherical pieces in physical foam because forming in two directions can change their surface dimensions.
</Note>

## Place the piece on the costume

Select the pattern and click **Place**. Set its **Foam position (mm)** and **Foam rotation (degrees)** for X, Y, and Z. These settings control its placement in the 3D view independently of the paper layout.

Use the 3D camera to check the piece from the front, side, and back. Adjust placement until the preview sits where you want it on the costume.

## Form a whole stack

When the selected pattern belongs to a foam layer, **Form** and **Place** apply to the stack's base pattern. All patterns in the stack follow that forming and placement. **Thickness** and **Edge Style** still apply to the individual selected pattern.

Change the base's forming after composing the stack to check that raised shapes stay in the intended locations. Continue with [Layers and materials](/foam-preview/layers-and-materials).
