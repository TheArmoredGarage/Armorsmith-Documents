An **attachment** is a costume part placed on your avatar from a [workspace](/interface/workspaces-panel). The workspace holds the model and its patterns. The attachment gives that model a position, rotation, and scale on the avatar so you can check its fit.

An **attachment point** anchors the part to a body region, such as the head, shoulder, forearm, or calf. The part follows that point as you pose the avatar. You can adjust its placement after attaching it.

## Attach a workspace to your avatar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a workspace">
    [Import a model](/parts/importing-models), then select it in the **Workspaces Panel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an attachment point">
    Click **Attach**. A ghosted preview of the part follows your pointer. Click the attachment point on the avatar where the part belongs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Position and fit the part">
    Select the attached part in the **Costume viewport** or **Attachment Tree**. Use [**Translate**](/interface/translate-tool) (**T**), [**Rotate**](/interface/rotate-tool) (**R**), and [**Scale**](/interface/scale-tool) (**S**) to fit it to the avatar.

    Use the [Transform Panel](/interface/transform-panel) for exact values. Inspect the fit from several angles before moving on to the next part.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Start in the avatar's neutral **T-Pose** when fitting parts. After positioning them, use [posing](/avatar/posing) to check how the costume moves with the body.
</Tip>

## Select and manage attachments

The **Attachment Tree** lists your attached parts under their attachment points. Click a part's name to select it. Use its **Visible** checkbox to show or hide it.

If you attached a part to the wrong body region, right-click the selected part in the **Attachment Tree**, choose **Move Part**, and select another attachment point. Use **Delete Part** to remove the attachment from the avatar.

Deleting an attachment leaves its source workspace available. Deleting the workspace also removes its connected attachments.

## Attachment properties

Select an attachment to inspect its **Property Grid**. Use the **Transform Panel** for position, rotation, and scale.

| Property | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The label shown for the part in the Attachment Tree. |
| **Visible** | Whether the part is displayed. Hide it temporarily to inspect other parts or the avatar beneath it. |
| **Frozen** | Whether the part is excluded from viewport selection. Freeze a fitted part to avoid selecting it while working nearby. |
| **Color** | The part's display color. |
| **Opacity** | How transparent the part appears. Lower it to see the avatar through the part while fitting. |
| **DrawTextures** | Whether the part displays its textures. Turn it off to inspect the shape without texture detail. |
| **Wireframe** | Whether the mesh displays as wireframe. You can also toggle this with **W** in the Costume viewport. |
| **Thickness** | The source workspace's default material thickness. Attachments from that workspace use the same workspace setting. |
| **Mirror** | Whether a linked mirrored copy is attached on the other side of the avatar. |
| **Position, rotation, and scale** | Where the part sits, which way it faces, and its size. Adjust these in the Transform Panel or with the viewport tools. |

## Mirror an attached part

Mirroring is useful for paired costume pieces such as shoulder pads, bracers, and greaves. For example, fit a bracer to the left forearm, then enable **Mirror** to place its reflected copy on the right forearm.

Armorsmith uses the opposite attachment point when one is available. For an attachment point without an opposite partner, the reflected copy uses the same attachment point.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the original attachment">
    Select the part in the **Costume viewport** or **Attachment Tree**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle the mirror">
    Focus the **Costume viewport** and press **Shift+M**. You can also change the attachment's **Mirror** property in the Property Grid.

    Press **Shift+M** again to turn mirroring off and remove the mirrored copy from the avatar. The original remains attached.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the pair">
    Move, rotate, or scale the original attachment to update its linked mirror. Selecting the mirrored copy selects the original so you can adjust the pair together.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For different shapes or independent placement on each side, attach separate parts instead of using a linked mirror.

### Mirror several selected parts

**Shift+M** toggles the **Mirror** setting of each selected attachment. A part with mirroring off turns it on; a part with mirroring on turns it off. A selection with mixed settings keeps that per-part toggle behavior.

The shortcut acts on individual attached parts. Select the parts themselves when working with a group.

### Undo and redo mirroring

Press **Ctrl+Z** to undo a **Shift+M** action. Every affected attachment returns to its previous mirror setting in one step, even when you selected several parts. Press **Ctrl+Y** to redo that same action.

Changing your selection afterward does not change which attachments that undo or redo affects.

<Note>
**Shift+M** and the single-step undo/redo behavior are available in builds that include the updated mirror command. Earlier builds use **C** for the mirror shortcut. **M** remains the shortcut for **Measure**.
</Note>
