Use **File → Export** to take the fitted costume into another application. Save your editable costume first. These exports do not replace the costume project or its pattern work.

## Export the costume to FBX

1. Load a costume and avatar. Review the fitted parts and the pose you want to export.
2. Choose **File → Export → Costume to FBX...**.
3. Choose a name and save the `.fbx` file. Wait for export to finish.
4. Open the FBX in your destination application and check dimensions, orientation, and the exported meshes.

The command exports the avatar and costume pieces as separate meshes. Workspaces without attachments are also exported under **Unattached Pieces**; remove unused workspaces before exporting if you do not want those models included. It is available in the full version. A costume locked for export must be unlocked before exporting. This is a model export, not an editable Armorsmith project or a promise of an animated rig in the destination application.

<Note>
**Printable Figure** is disabled because it does not work. Miniature STL export is not available.
</Note>

## Share an editable costume

Use [**File → Publish Costume**](/interface/costume-files#publish-one-file-for-sharing) for a single costume bundle. It preserves editable costume data rather than producing an FBX model. The mobile/free-viewer scene is not included with this build's feature selection.
