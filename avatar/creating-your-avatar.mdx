<Info>
These instructions are for **Armorsmith Designer 2.1.1.0**. Earlier versions, including 2.1.0.10, can show different panels and labels.
</Info>

Your avatar is the body you fit costume parts around. In 2.1.1.0, the included male and female bodies use **Avatar 4**. Open **Avatar → Edit Avatar** to show avatar customization in **Workbench → Edit**.

## Before you start

![Avatar Guide Natural Waist step with illustrated tape placement, 78.3 cm value, and Back and Next buttons.](/images/avatar-guide-step-2110.png)

*The illustrated guide explains each measurement. Changes apply as you go.*


Have a flexible tape measure and your recorded measurements. Wear the base layers and supportive garment intended for the costume. Set your preferred units in [Preferences](/interface/preferences).

Save an existing setup before changing bodies or resetting it. An avatar is a fitting reference; check critical dimensions and make a physical test fit before committing to final materials.

## Start with Avatar Guide

On the Avatar tab, click **Avatar Guide** for illustrated measurement steps. Check the units and body choice, enter each measurement, and use **Back**, **Next**, and **Finish** to move through the guide. **Changes apply as you go**; closing the dialog does not mean the earlier entries were only a preview.

<Warning>
Save a useful avatar before changing **Base Avatar** inside the guide. Switching that choice starts the selected body setup again. It differs from changing the base in the main customization panel while preserving a fitted profile.
</Warning>

After the guide, inspect the body from several directions and refine the detailed measurements below. The panel's separate task/fitting guide provides workflow advice; it is not the same measurement-entry dialog.

## Create a built-in avatar

1. On the **Avatar** tab, choose **Male** or **Female** in **Base avatar**. Choose the starting body closest to your proportions. Switching bodies transfers the existing measurement profile and supported appearance settings; inspect the resulting shape rather than assuming a reset.
2. Set **Age** and the **Body build** controls as a starting shape. **Body mass** and **Muscularity** are relative shape controls, not weight in kilograms or pounds.
3. Enter **Standing height** with units, such as `175 cm`, and press **Enter**. Set height before refining other dimensions. Entering a height makes later age changes preserve your chosen standing height.
4. Open **Measurements** and enter lengths using the highlighted guides as your endpoints. Set compound lengths before their component segments. See [Body measurements](/avatar/body-measurements).
5. Open **Circumferences** and match the levels shown by the rings. Compare front and side views while adjusting size and shape. Use [Chest and breast shape](/avatar/chest-and-breast-shape) for torso and breast controls.
6. Use **Head** for head dimensions and face shape. Use **Hands** for the available hand and finger measurements. Availability depends on the loaded avatar rig.
7. Inspect the body from the front, side, and back. Recheck values after larger shape changes, then choose **Avatar → Save As** to save an `.avatar` file.

## Find the right tab

| Tab | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Avatar** | Base avatar, standing height, age, body build, photo fitting, appearance, and viewport display. |
| **Measurements** | Lengths, proportions, and body measurement guides. |
| **Circumferences** | Body rings, local shape, and the available breast-shape controls. |
| **Head** | Head circumference, width/depth dimensions, and face shaping. |
| **Hands** | Available hand/finger measurements and left/right hand framing. |

Older built-in avatars use collapsible **Full Body**, **Measurements**, **Circumferences**, and **Details** sections. Their layout and detail controls differ from Avatar 4. Imported bodies have their own [custom-avatar workflow](/avatar/custom-avatars).

## Start from photographs

On **Avatar**, under **Photo fitting**, click **Fit avatar from photos…**. Supply a front photo and your standing height; a side photo helps estimate depth. Review the detected points and measurements before applying them. Follow [Fit an avatar from photos](/avatar/photo-fitting).

**Show reference image** displays an image for visual comparison. Loading a reference image alone does not run photo fitting or resize the body.

## Refine measurements in the viewport

While editing the avatar, hover over a measurement control to identify its guide. Drag an available guide handle to preview an adjustment, then release it to commit the change. Check the value in the panel. A locked measurement cannot be dragged; unlock it first if you intend to change it.

On **Head**, open **More head measurements** for **Head Width (side to side)** and **Head Length (front to back)**. Editing one keeps the other dimension while circumference changes with the shape.

Use the lock beside a measurement to preserve it while related dimensions change. Locking is undoable. If a combination cannot be applied, read the panel's error and review the affected values rather than forcing every field to a new target.

## Check and save before fitting parts

Use **Show Costume** to hide attachments while you inspect the body. Check clothing, footwear, head shape, and the intended hairstyle. Choose **Avatar → Save As** to keep separate setups for different wearers or clothing.

Then [attach a costume part](/parts/attaching-parts), adjust its fit, and use [Fit Checker](/parts/fit-checker). For recovery and reset choices, see [Avatar troubleshooting](/avatar/avatar-troubleshooting).
