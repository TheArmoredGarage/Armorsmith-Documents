Your avatar is the foundation of every project in Armorsmith Designer. It is a 3D human figure built from your actual body measurements, and every costume part you attach is sized and scaled against it. Taking a few minutes to create an accurate avatar means your printed templates will fit you — not some generic mannequin.

## Why the avatar matters

When you attach a costume model to your avatar, Armorsmith Designer uses the avatar's proportions to determine how large each pattern piece should be. An avatar that matches your body produces templates you can print and assemble with minimal adjustment. Skipping this step, or entering rough estimates, shifts the fitting work from the software to the cutting table.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Avatar Editor">
    In the top menu bar, click **Avatar**, then select **Edit Avatar**. The Avatar Editor panel opens in the centre of the workspace, and your current avatar (or the default placeholder) appears in the viewport.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your body measurements">
    Fill in each measurement field in the editor panel. Work through the fields from top to bottom — Height first, then circumferences and lengths. See the [Body Measurements guide](./body-measurements) for a full list of fields and tips on how to measure accurately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your measurements">
    Click **Apply** to generate the avatar. Armorsmith Designer processes your inputs and reshapes the 3D figure to match your proportions. This usually takes only a second or two.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result in the viewport">
    Look at the updated avatar in the main viewport. Rotate the view to check that the overall shape looks proportional and matches your expectations. If something looks obviously wrong — an extremely elongated limb, a distorted torso — go back into **Edit Avatar** and double-check the affected measurement field.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Enter your measurements as precisely as possible. A single bad value — for example, a height entered in inches when the field expects centimetres — can cause severe distortion or trigger the black screen bug, where the viewport goes completely black. If this happens, go to **Avatar → Reset Avatar**, then restart Armorsmith Designer. See the [Body Measurements guide](./body-measurements) for details on setting your preferred units before you begin.
</Warning>

Once your avatar looks right, you are ready to attach costume model files and start generating templates. You can return to **Edit Avatar** at any time to update your measurements — for example, after losing or gaining weight, or when building a costume for someone else.
