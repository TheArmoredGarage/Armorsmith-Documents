The **Avatar Customization** panel allows you to customize the appearance and measurements of your avatar. Use this panel to create an avatar that closely matches your own body measurements before fitting costume pieces.

Changes made in the Avatar Customization panel are immediately reflected on the avatar in the Work Area.

<Frame>
  ![Avatar Customization panel](/images/Avatar-Customization.png)
</Frame>

## Full Body

The **Full Body** section contains the general settings for your avatar, including height, gender, appearance, and display options.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Height Estimate">
    Sets the approximate overall height of the avatar.

    Adjusting the height estimate scales the avatar while maintaining its current body proportions.

    <Tip>
      Set your **Height Estimate** before adjusting individual measurements. This provides a good starting point for customizing the rest of the avatar.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Gender">
    Selects the base body type used by the avatar.

    Changing the gender will update the underlying avatar proportions and available appearance options.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Hair">
    Selects the hairstyle displayed on the avatar.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Facial Hair">
    Selects the facial hair displayed on the avatar.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Hair Color">
    Sets the color of the avatar's hair and facial hair.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Skin Color">
    Adjusts the skin tone of the avatar.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Eye Color">
    Sets the eye color of the avatar.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Underwear Color">
    Sets the color of the avatar's underwear.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Enable Fullbody Color">
    Overrides the avatar's individual colors with a single full-body color.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Show Costume">
    Controls whether currently loaded costume pieces are displayed while editing the avatar.

    Disable this option when you want an unobstructed view of the avatar and its measurements.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Measurements

The **Measurements** section allows you to fine-tune the dimensions and proportions of the avatar.

Measurement guides are displayed directly on the avatar in the Work Area, making it easier to see which part of the body is affected by each adjustment.

Measurements are divided into **Compound Measurements** and **Individual Measurements**.

### Compound Measurements

Compound measurements represent larger body dimensions that depend on several parts of the avatar.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Torso Length">
    Adjusts the overall length of the torso.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Arm Length">
    Adjusts the total length of the arm while maintaining the relationship between the upper arm, forearm, and hand.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Inseam">
    Adjusts the total inside leg length.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Compound measurements are useful when you have a real-world body measurement but do not know the dimensions of each individual body segment.
</Tip>

### Individual Measurements

Individual measurements provide more precise control over specific parts of the avatar.

These include measurements such as:

- Neck Length
- Shoulder Width
- Elbow Length
- Forearm Length
- Knee Length
- Lower Leg Length
- Hand Length
- Foot Length
- Crown to Chin

Adjusting an individual measurement modifies that specific portion of the avatar.

### Reset Measurement

The **Reset** button beside a measurement restores that measurement to its default value.

This can be useful if you want to undo an individual proportion change without resetting the entire avatar.

## Circumferences

The **Circumferences** section controls measurements around the body, such as the head, neck, chest, waist, arms, and legs.

Each circumference may provide two controls:

**Size** changes the overall circumference of that part of the body.

**Shape** changes how the circumference is distributed without significantly changing its overall size.

For example, two people may have the same chest circumference while having different chest shapes. The **Shape** control allows the avatar to better represent these differences.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Head Circumference">
    Adjusts the circumference of the head.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Neck Circumference">
    Adjusts the circumference of the neck.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  For the most accurate results, enter your real-world measurements where possible instead of relying only on the visual appearance of the avatar.
</Tip>

## Measurement Guides

When editing the avatar, measurement guides are displayed over the model in the Work Area.

These guides show where each measurement is being taken and provide a visual reference for how the avatar's dimensions relate to the values in the Avatar Customization panel.

The guides automatically update as the avatar's proportions are changed.

<Warning>
  Enter your measurements as precisely as possible. A single bad value — for example, a height entered in inches when the field expects centimetres — can cause severe distortion or trigger the black screen bug, where the viewport goes completely black. If this happens, go to **Avatar → Reset Avatar**, then restart Armorsmith Designer. See the [Body Measurements guide](./body-measurements) for details on setting your preferred units before you begin.
</Warning>

Once your avatar looks right, you are ready to attach costume model files and start generating templates. You can return to **Edit Avatar** at any time to update your measurements — for example, after losing or gaining weight, or when building a costume for someone else.
