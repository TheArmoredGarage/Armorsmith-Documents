Measure the person who will wear the costume, then enter those values in **Avatar → Edit Avatar**. The highlighted guides on the avatar are your reference for each field's location and endpoints.

## Prepare your measurements

Use a flexible tape measure and ask someone to help with measurements you cannot see clearly. Wear the base layers and supportive undergarments you intend to use beneath the costume. Measure in a relaxed, upright stance.

For circumferences, keep the tape level and in contact with the body without compressing it. Breathe normally. Measure twice and repeat any reading that differs noticeably.

Set the unit display in [**Settings → Preferences**](/interface/preferences). Label the units in your notes. For example, **90 cm** and **35.4 in** describe approximately the same circumference; **90 in** describes a very different body.

<Tip>
Measure the body or its intended base layer first. Allow for armor thickness, padding, and movement when [fitting costume parts](/parts/attaching-parts), rather than adding an arbitrary amount to every avatar measurement.
</Tip>

## Enter a measurement

1. Choose **Base avatar** and set **Standing height** on the **Avatar** tab before fine-tuning.
2. Open **Measurements** or **Circumferences**.
3. Hover over the field to highlight its guide.
4. Identify the guide's endpoints or the height of its circumference ring.
5. Enter your value and press **Enter**. Check the value displayed after entry.
6. Inspect the avatar from more than one angle.

Sliders provide live adjustments. Numeric fields let you enter a measured value. Use the nearby **Reset** button to restore a field's default.

## Overall height

**Standing height** changes the overall height. Enter a value with units and press **Enter**. Locked measurements are preserved while related dimensions adjust. Set height first and recheck the other dimensions after changing it. On Avatar 4, entering height also keeps that chosen height during later age changes.

## Find the measurement you need

Avatar 4 groups measurements by body region and uses maker-facing names. The guide on the body determines the endpoints or ring level. Do not substitute a clothing measurement with different endpoints.

| Region | Visible fields to look for | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Head & Neck** | **Head**, **Head height**, **Collar** | Head ring, crown-to-chin span, and neck ring. |
| **Shoulders & Back** | **Shoulder width**, **Single shoulder**, **Across back**, **Back length**, **Armhole** | Match the highlighted endpoints; shoulder width differs from the rig's shoulder-joint spacing. |
| **Chest & Bust** | **Chest** or **Full bust**, **High bust**, **Underbust**, **Across chest** | Use each ring's indicated level. Female bodies label the chest measurement Full bust. |
| **Waist & Hips** | **Natural waist**, **Trouser waist**, **Seat** | Keep the waist and upper/lower hip levels separate. |
| **Arms** | **Sleeve**, **Shoulder to elbow**, **Elbow to wrist**, **Upper arm (top)**, **Bicep**, **Elbow**, **Forearm**, **Wrist** | Distinguish lengths from rings and check the elbow position. |
| **Legs** | **Outseam**, **Rise**, **Inseam**, **Crotch to knee**, **Knee to ankle**, **Thigh**, **Knee**, **Calf**, **Ankle** | Check both total length and where the knee sits. |
| **Advanced (rig controls)** | **Shoulder joints (rig)**, **Torso length (rig)**, **Neck length (rig)** | These describe the rig rather than replacing the practical measurement guides. |

Available fields depend on the loaded body. Older avatars can display longer internal names such as **Elbow Length** for shoulder-to-elbow and **Low Hip Circumference** for the seat ring.

## Compound lengths

Set totals before fine-tuning their component segments. **Inseam** combines upper and lower leg segments. **Outseam** relates rise and inseam; **Sleeve** relates shoulder-to-elbow and elbow-to-wrist with the shoulder-cap allowance shown by the guide.

Changing a total can adjust its components. A correct total can still place an elbow or knee incorrectly, so inspect joint positions and recheck the component values. Locks constrain which related measurements can change. Read the displayed relationship and any error when a requested combination cannot be applied.

## Circumference size and shape

**Size** changes the circumference setting. **Shape** changes the balance between side-to-side width and front-to-back depth. Compare front and side views when the number looks right but the region is too broad or deep.

The controls approximate a body cross-section. A circumference value alone cannot describe every contour of a real body. On Avatar 4, additional regional shape controls can affect nearby rings together; recheck their displayed values after editing.

### Chest, full bust, and underbust

Measure **Full bust** around the fullest breast area while wearing the intended support, and **Underbust** around the ribcage beneath it. Use the actual guide levels for chest/high-bust readings. Avatar 4 provides [approximate cup presets and breast-shape controls](/avatar/chest-and-breast-shape); these are starting shapes, not a substitute for tape measurements.

## Keep a measurement record

Record the wearer, date, units, clothing setup, and readings together. Update the record when the intended base layers or the wearer's dimensions change.

Save a matching [avatar file](/avatar/saving-and-reusing-avatars) so you can return to that setup. If the numbers are plausible but the shape still looks wrong, see [Avatar troubleshooting](/avatar/avatar-troubleshooting).

## Preserve a measurement while changing proportions

Click the lock beside an editable measurement to preserve it while related dimensions change. Locked fields stay disabled until you unlock them. A viewport drag also respects the lock. Use **Edit → Undo** to reverse a lock change or committed body edit.

Hover over a field to identify its guide, or drag an available guide handle in the viewport and read the resulting value in the panel. Check compound lengths after changing a component and read any constraint error before continuing.

The **Head** tab repeats head circumference and provides additional head dimensions. **Hands** contains the available hand and finger measurements; detailed fingers require the five-finger rig. For an estimated starting pass, use [photo fitting](/avatar/photo-fitting).
