## title: "Body Measurements Guide for Armorsmith Designer" sidebarTitle: "Measurements" description: "A complete guide to every body measurement field in Armorsmith Designer and how to take each measurement accurately for precise printable templates."

The measurements you enter in Armorsmith Designer flow directly into every template the software generates. If your chest circumference is off by two centimetres, every breastplate piece will be off by a proportional amount. Getting these numbers right before you begin saves time at the build stage and reduces wasted materials.

## Before you start

<Tip>
  Set your preferred unit system — millimetres, centimetres, or inches — in **Preferences** before opening the Avatar Editor. Changing units after you have entered measurements can cause values to be misread and may trigger display issues.
</Tip>

Take your measurements wearing tight-fitting clothing (such as a base layer or form-fitting T-shirt and leggings) or directly on skin. Loose clothing adds bulk and throws off circumference measurements. Use a **soft fabric tape measure** rather than a rigid ruler — it follows your body's curves for accurate circumference readings.

Have a second person help where possible, especially for back-width and arm measurements. Self-measuring in front of a mirror works, but a helper improves accuracy.

## Measurement fields

| Field | What to measure |
| --- | --- |
| **Height** | Stand upright against a wall without shoes. Measure from the floor to the top of your head. |
| **Chest circumference** | Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, keeping it level across your back. Breathe normally — don't puff out or suck in. |
| **Waist circumference** | Measure around your natural waist, which is the narrowest point of your torso, typically an inch or two above your navel. |
| **Hip circumference** | Measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat, usually 7–9 inches below your natural waist. |
| **Arm length** | Measure from the bony point of your shoulder, down the outside of a slightly bent arm, to your wrist bone. |
| **Inseam** | Measure from your crotch — the point where your legs meet — straight down to the floor (or to your ankle bone if you want to exclude footwear). |
| **Head circumference** | Wrap the tape around your head just above your eyebrows and ears, at the widest point. |
| **Shoulder width** | Measure straight across your back from the bony tip of one shoulder to the tip of the other. |

## Tips for accurate measuring

- **Keep the tape level.** Especially for circumferences, let the tape sit parallel to the floor all the way around. A tape that dips at the back gives a smaller reading than your actual size.
- **Don't pull the tape tight.** It should sit snugly against your body but not compress the skin or clothing.
- **Measure twice.** Write down the first reading, take the tape off, re-measure, and compare. If the two readings differ by more than a few millimetres, take a third measurement and use the middle value.
- **Use consistent units.** Mixing inches and centimetres across different fields is the most common source of errors. Confirm your unit setting in Preferences before you start and don't change it mid-session.

## The black screen issue

<Warning>
  Entering an implausible value — such as a height of 5 when the field expects centimetres, or a waist measurement larger than the height — can cause the avatar to distort severely or make the viewport go completely black. If this happens:

  1. Go to **Avatar → Reset Avatar** to restore all measurements to their defaults.
  2. Close and restart Armorsmith Designer.
  3. Reopen **Avatar → Edit Avatar** and re-enter your measurements carefully, confirming your unit setting first.
</Warning>

Once all fields are filled in and your unit system is confirmed, click **Apply** in the Avatar Editor to generate your avatar. See [Creating Your Avatar](./creating-your-avatar) for the full setup walkthrough.
