Once your avatar's body measurements are set, you can go further and personalize how it looks. Armorsmith Designer lets you tweak facial features, hairstyle, body build, and skin tone so the figure in your viewport feels like a genuine stand-in for you (or whoever you are building a costume for). This makes it much easier to visualize how a costume will actually look on a real person rather than a blank mannequin.

<Note>
  Appearance customization is purely visual. None of the options on this page — facial features, hairstyle, body shape, or skin tone — change the underlying measurements used to calculate template sizes. Only the measurement fields in the **Edit Avatar** panel affect pattern generation. You can change appearance settings at any time without any risk to your project's accuracy.
</Note>

## Accessing appearance options

All appearance settings live in the same place as your measurements:

1. Click **Avatar** in the top menu bar.
2. Select **Edit Avatar**.
3. In the Avatar Editor panel, switch to the **Appearance** tab (or scroll past the measurement fields, depending on your version).

## What you can customize

<Accordion title="Facial features">
  Adjust the shape and proportions of the face — including jawline, nose, and eye spacing — to better match the wearer's look. These changes are reflected in the viewport in real time as you drag the sliders or enter values.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Hairstyle">
  Choose from a set of preset hairstyle options to give the avatar a more lifelike silhouette. This is especially useful when designing headpieces, helmets, or hoods, where hair volume affects how a piece sits on the head visually.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Body build / shape">
  A slider lets you shift the avatar's visible body shape along a spectrum from slim to broad. This adjusts the visual silhouette without altering the circumference or length measurements you entered. Use it to better represent the wearer's natural build so costume parts look proportional in the viewport.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Skin tone">
  Select a skin tone from the available swatches. This affects the avatar's surface colour in the viewport and has no bearing on pattern output.
</Accordion>

## Applying your changes

After adjusting any appearance options, click **Apply** to update the avatar in the viewport. You can return to this tab and make further adjustments at any time — even after costume parts have been attached. Because these settings are decoupled from measurements, changing them will never invalidate your templates.
