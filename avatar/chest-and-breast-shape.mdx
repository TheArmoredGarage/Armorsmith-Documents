Measure while wearing the supportive garment or base layer intended for the costume. Record **Underbust** around the ribcage beneath the breasts and **Full bust** around the fullest area. Keep the tape level without compressing the body.

## Avatar 4 workflow

Open **Avatar → Edit Avatar → Circumferences**. Fit the overall height and torso proportions first. **Breast shape** is shown for a Female Avatar 4 aged 18 or older when the loaded asset includes the required breast shapes.

1. Match the torso rings to your measurements and check front and side views.
2. Enable **Adjust breasts separately from torso** for the independent breast layer. **Fit torso before breasts** uses torso circumference fitting while preserving breast shape on the resized chest.
3. Choose **Cup system** (**US / Canada** or **UK**) and an approximate **Cup size** starting preset.
4. Adjust **Spacing** and **Support** to match the intended garment's silhouette.
5. Open **More breast controls** only if needed. Tune one control at a time, checking the front and profile views.
6. Inspect the displayed approximate ring values, compare with your tape measurements, then show the costume and [check clearance](/parts/fit-checker).

| Control | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Cup size** | An approximate starting shape calibrated using the current underbust at selection. |
| **Spacing** | Closer together or farther apart; the centre preserves authored spacing. |
| **Support** | The breast layer's supported shape. Compare it with the actual garment you plan to wear. |
| **Fine size** | Local size adjustment; 100% is neutral. Editing it changes the cup preset to Custom. |
| **Firmness** | A blend between authored soft and firm shapes; 50% is neutral. It is not a measured tissue property. |
| **Width**, **Projection** | Local shape adjustments; 100% preserves the local shape. |

Cup letters vary by sizing system and brand. A preset does not guarantee a particular bra fit or final bust circumference. Later underbust edits keep breast size fixed, so recheck the silhouette and choose or tune the preset again if needed.

If a preset is outside the supported shape range, read the message and use **Fine size** and shape tuning. Do not shrink an accurate ribcage solely to make a breastplate fit; adjust the part instead.

## Older built-in avatars

Older bodies use **Details → Torso** with independent **Breast Size**, **Breast Height**, and **Breast Projection** controls. Their midpoint preserves the base shape for that control. These sliders are shape amounts, not tape-measure values or cup sizes.

Older files made with the combined Breast Size slider are converted to independent settings to retain the saved shape. Leave the converted settings in place unless you intend to change that shape. Save a copy before refining an older avatar.

See [Body measurements](/avatar/body-measurements) and [Saving and reusing avatars](/avatar/saving-and-reusing-avatars).
