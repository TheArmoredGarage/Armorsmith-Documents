<Info>
These instructions are for **Armorsmith Designer 2.1.1.0**. Earlier versions, including 2.1.0.10, can show different panels and labels.
</Info>

Use clear lighting while fitting a costume. Appearance changes do not alter physical size, pattern scale, or clearance. Use [Measure](/interface/measurement-tool) and [Fit Checker](/parts/fit-checker) to inspect geometry.

## Start with a clear fitting view

1. Open **Settings → Preferences → Rendering**.
2. Keep lighting enabled and turn **Depth Of Field** off when checking fine edges.
3. Turn bloom off if glow makes boundaries difficult to see.
4. Inspect the part from several camera angles before judging its shape.

The Preferences tab exposes display switches, not every room-tuning value. It no longer provides the old table of BloomIntensity, blur radius, and global-intensity fields. See [Preferences](/interface/preferences#rendering).

## Keep fitting checks consistent

Use the same camera and pose when comparing appearance changes. For a slow viewport, simplify the view or disable costly effects in Preferences. Lighting and rendering quality do not establish fit or replace a physical test.

Room-specific lighting authoring and Environment Settings are internal development tools, not customer controls. Use the Rendering switches described above for the available display options.
